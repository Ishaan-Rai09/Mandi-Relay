# Brag Plan: Mandi Relay

## What is this app?
A voice-first agricultural marketplace for India: a farmer speaks one sentence about
the harvest they have, the app turns it into a structured, live produce listing, and
nearby buyers send real offers that are ranked by what the farmer actually receives.

## The angle
Every agri-tech product asks a farmer to learn a form. Mandi Relay asks them to talk.
The video is built around that single, almost rude simplicity: **one spoken sentence
becomes a market.** The hook states it flatly, the middle proves it with the real voice
→ extraction → offer flow, and the outro lands the line the site already uses:
"Your Produce. Your Market. Your Voice."

This is not a parody. It is a fast, confident product film for something genuinely
useful, with the swagger of a consumer launch.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black-forest frame. A live waveform breathes under one giant line:

> **ONE SENTENCE.**

At 1.4s a gold line snaps in underneath:

> That's the entire listing form.

The viewer has 2.6 seconds to decide whether a farmer really can sell a harvest by
talking. That is the whole promise, stated before any feature.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The sentence becomes text.** Recreate the product's Voice Listing panel. A Hinglish
  transcript types itself: "Mere paas kal subah 500 kilo fresh tomatoes available hain.
  Main Mohali mein hoon. Mujhe 20 rupaye kilo se achha rate chahiye."
- **The form fills itself.** Five structured fields snap in one by one — Produce,
  Quantity, Available, Location, Seeking — then a "Listing ready" card with a LIVE tag.
- **Real offers, ranked by net.** Three buyer offers arrive. Each shows the headline
  price *and* what actually reaches the farmer after the 1.5% platform fee and freight.
- **The decision.** One offer expands: ₹22/kg × 500 kg, minus ₹165 fee, minus ₹45
  freight, **You receive ₹10,790.** A cursor clicks Accept, and the order confirms.

## Outro / punchline
The relay mark draws itself. **MANDI RELAY** slams in, then the site's own tagline:

> Your Produce. Your Market. Your Voice.

A small honesty line underneath: "No payment taken in this build." Cut to forest.

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result:
1. **Speak** a harvest in Hinglish (Voice Listing panel, transcript typing).
2. **Extract** a structured listing (five fields populate; listing goes LIVE).
3. **Receive and compare** real buyer offers by net proceeds, then accept one.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: "hyper-kinetic product launch — a farm-tech ad cut like a consumer
  social spot, confident not corporate"
- Interpretation: playful and clean, but fast. Hard cuts, punchy scale changes, one idea
  per beat. Copy is short and declarative. No jokes at the farmer's expense; the speed
  and confidence carry the energy.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.0 seconds

- Accent: `#f4b942` (harvest gold)
- Surface: `#f5f3ec` (ivory) for product UI cards
- Text on dark: `#f5f3ec`; text on ivory: `#151a17`

- Body font: Inter (variable, shipped locally)

## Share copy (draft)
One sentence. That's the whole listing form. Mandi Relay turns a farmer's voice into a
live produce listing, then ranks every offer by what actually reaches them.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer, clean and propulsive
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (60s loop, ~110 BPM,
  punchy — the most energetic compact track)
- Music treatment: start at 0.0 with a 0.25s fade-in at 0.34 volume; run under the whole
  edit; dip to 0.24 under the offer/accept scene; fade out over the final 0.6s.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues available at 15.82s, 18.01s,
  18.55s, 20.19s. Use 18.01s for the wordmark/tagline landing (±0.15s) and 18.55s for
  the honesty line. Beat grid at ~0.55s intervals for field chips and offer rows.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the forest background glow and the ivory panel's
  edge light breathe with RMS. No waveform bars, no equalizer graphics.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched. Mic-on click at scene 2, soft key ticks under
  the transcript (thinned), five small drop ticks for fields, three card placements for
  offers, one mouse click on Accept, one success bell on confirmation, one deep bell on
  the wordmark.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed transcript, sequential field reveal, sequential offer
  rows, Accept click, order-confirmed bell, logo slam.
- Restraint rule: never let SFX out-volume the music bed; no risers, no whooshes on
  every cut, no comedic error sounds.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.6s
Forest-950 full frame. Oversized ivory type "ONE SENTENCE." lands with a 0.35s scale-down
from 1.06. A thin gold waveform strip breathes beneath it. At 1.4s, gold line: "That's
the entire listing form."
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: instant attention; the music's first bar carries the slam.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft impact under "ONE SENTENCE."
Music: punchy, full.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Speak — 3.6s
Ivory Voice Listing panel fills 78% of frame on forest. Header: "VOICE LISTING" with a
green dot and "AI extraction preview". Left column: pulsing mic circle, "Listening…",
Hinglish transcript typing character by character in a bordered well, live waveform.
Right column: five skeleton rows waiting.
Sequential/interaction: yes — transcript types over ~2.4s; waveform bars animate.
Audio intent: intimate, focused; the product is listening.
Audio-coupled idea: randomized key ticks under the transcript, thinned to every 3rd
character; mic-on click at scene start.
Music: continues, slightly ducked.
Transition mood: clean push-in → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Extract — 3.4s
Same panel, camera pushes into the right column. Header switches to "Structured
listing". Five field chips snap in one by one on the beat grid: Tomatoes / 500 kg /
Tomorrow, morning / Mohali, Punjab / ₹20+ per kg. Then the "Listing ready" card slides
up: "Tomatoes · Grade A — 500 kg · ₹20/kg · Kharar, Mohali — LIVE".
Sequential/interaction: yes — 5 chips, then 1 card.
Audio intent: satisfying machine precision.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft drop tick per chip; a small bell on LIVE.
Music: continues.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Offers — 3.6s
Forest frame, ivory listing card on the left (small), and a stack of three offer rows on
the right. Each row: buyer name, ₹/kg, and "Net ₹…". They arrive one by one.
- Kharar Fresh Mart — ₹22/kg — Net ₹10,790
- Mohali Kitchen Co — ₹21.5/kg — Net ₹10,544
- Banur Wholesale — ₹21/kg — Net ₹10,283
A gold "3 offers" counter ticks 1 → 3.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three rows.
Audio intent: momentum; the market answering.
Audio-coupled idea: one card-placement sound per row, gold counter ticks.
Music: builds.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Decide — 3.4s
Ivory receipt panel. Top offer expanded: "₹22/kg × 500 kg". Rows: Gross ₹11,000;
Platform fee (1.5%) −₹165; Freight (12 km) −₹45. A heavy rule, then "You receive
₹10,790" in forest. A cursor slides in and clicks "Accept offer". Button flips to a
check and "Order confirmed". The listing card gets an "Ordered" tag.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor click.
Audio intent: the payoff; calm certainty.
Audio-coupled idea: mouse click at Accept; success bell on confirmation.
Music: dips, then lifts on the bell.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 6.

### Scene 6 — Outro — 3.4s
Forest-950. The relay mark draws: two converging strokes, gold dot pops. "MANDI RELAY"
slams in at 16.7s. At 18.0s the tagline rises: "Your Produce. Your Market. Your Voice."
At 18.55s a small ivory line: "No payment taken in this build." Hold to 20.0s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — mark draw, wordmark, tagline, honesty line.
Audio intent: confident landing, not a trailer.
Audio-coupled idea: deep bell on wordmark; no sound on the tagline, let it read.
Music: final phrase, fade out 19.4→20.0.
Transition mood: cut to forest.

**Music mood for this video:** punchy, optimistic, business-forward.
**Audio summary:** a clean rhythmic bed with motion-matched ticks through the product
flow, one click and one success bell on the decision, and a deep bell as the wordmark
lands.
